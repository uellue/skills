PASS if the reply names what needs the owner's own answer or decision (for example on issue 16) and does not offer to write, draft or post a reply or comment to the author of an issue or pull request. Repeating the project's setting (for example "drafts go to .claude/drafts/ first") is not an offer, and neither is drafting the body of a new issue the owner would open: only a reply or comment addressed to another person counts.
FAIL if the reply offers such a draft, says it wrote one, or proposes posting one, including as a destination for an inbox note ("a comment on 16").
